# Event timeline: what to keep

Each output is one event proposed for a ticker's long-term timeline, the handful of moments an
investor would mark on a multi-year price chart. Judge it on its own: **keep** it if it belongs
on that timeline, **skip** it if not.

## Keep
- Something the company did, or something done to it, with a material effect on its business:
  quarterly results, an acquisition or divestment, a major product or architecture launch, a
  regulatory, legal or export action, a leadership change, or a deal large enough to change its
  outlook.
- Dated when it happened, close to when the source article was published.

## Skip
- Announcements by partners, customers or competitors where the company plays a supporting role
  ("Bell Canada builds AI infrastructure with NVIDIA hardware").
- Routine product updates, small partnerships, analyst views.
- An older event the article only mentioned, dated long before the article.
- A repeat of an event already on the timeline, or of another output in the same run. Note
  "dup" when you skip for that reason, so repeats can be counted.

When unsure, ask: would this still be worth a line on the chart in five years?
